# v0.30 — anchored quick navigation

- Adds a mobile quick-navigation trigger anchored to the right edge of every contextual bottom bar.
- Tap to open a blurred destination fan, then tap Create, Visuals, Library, or Settings.
- Press/drag from the trigger to highlight a destination and release to jump without an extra tap.
- Keeps existing contextual bottom tabs intact while making top-level area changes reachable one-handed.
- Create still lands on Output; Visuals lands on Models; Settings lands on Connection/current settings surface.
- Includes reduced-motion handling and keyboard-accessible tap behavior.
